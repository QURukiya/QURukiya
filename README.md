## About Me

I am a graduate researcher in **Geography at the University of Iowa** with an academic background in **Civil Engineering**.

My research lies at the intersection of **remote sensing, hydroclimate science, geospatial analysis, and machine learning**. I work with satellite observations, reanalysis products, and ground-based measurements to investigate environmental variability and improve the monitoring and prediction of soil moisture, agricultural drought, and related climate hazards.

My transition from Civil Engineering to Geography has shaped my interest in combining **physical understanding of environmental systems with geospatial data science and GeoAI**.

## Featured Research

### SMAP L4 × ERA5-Land Soil Moisture Analysis — Iowa

A reproducible pixel-wise assessment of satellite and reanalysis-based surface soil moisture across Iowa, including:

- SMAP L4–ERA5-Land comparison
- spatial and seasonal agreement analysis
- soil-moisture drought diagnostics
- trend and change-point analysis
- atmospheric controls of dataset disagreement
- soil-moisture memory and persistence
- hydroclimate regime classification
- Random Forest next-month prediction
